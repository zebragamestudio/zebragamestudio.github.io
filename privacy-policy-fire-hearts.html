Zebra Game Studio (“we”, “us”, or “our”) develops and publishes the mobile game Fire in Ardent Hearts (the “Game”).

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you install and use our Game. We respect your privacy and aim to provide a safe and transparent gaming experience.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Personal Information

The Game does not require users to create an account or complete any registration.

We do not directly request or collect personal information such as:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Physical address
  • Precise location
  • Contacts
  • Payment information

1.2 Device Information

The Game may process limited technical information associated with your device, including:

  • Device ID or similar device identifiers
  • Advertising ID
  • Device model and manufacturer
  • Operating system and version
  • Application version

Such information may be used for basic application functionality, compatibility, stability, and technical maintenance.

Advertising identifiers are not used to display personalized advertisements or track users for advertising purposes.

1.3 Local Game Data

Fire in Ardent Hearts may store gameplay-related information locally on your device.

This information may include:

  • Game progress
  • Current and completed levels
  • Best scores
  • Gameplay sessions
  • Unlocked achievements
  • Game settings

This information is used to maintain gameplay progress and provide game-related functionality.

Local gameplay data is not transmitted to our servers.

2. Offline Gameplay

Fire in Ardent Hearts is designed to function without an active internet connection.

Players can access the main gameplay, complete levels, improve scores, and unlock achievements offline.

An internet connection is not required for normal gameplay.

3. Third-Party Services

The Game does not integrate third-party advertising networks, analytics platforms, or social media tracking services.

In particular, the Game does not use:

  • Google AdMob
  • Firebase Analytics
  • Facebook SDK
  • Yandex Metrica
  • Third-party advertising trackers

Fire in Ardent Hearts does not display advertisements and does not use third-party analytics to monitor player behavior.

4. How We Use Information

Information handled by the Game may be used for the following purposes:

  • To operate and maintain the Game
  • To save local gameplay progress and achievements
  • To maintain compatibility with supported devices
  • To support application stability and functionality
  • To identify and resolve technical issues

We do not sell, rent, or trade user information to third parties.

5. Advertising

Fire in Ardent Hearts does not contain advertisements.

The Game does not display banner advertisements, interstitial advertisements, rewarded advertisements, or personalized advertising content.

6. In-App Purchases

The Game does not offer in-app purchases, paid subscriptions, or other paid features.

We do not collect or store payment card details, billing information, or financial data.

7. Data Storage and Retention

Gameplay information, including scores, achievements, settings, and session history, may be stored locally on your device.

This information may remain available until the Game is uninstalled or its application data is cleared.

Zebra Game Studio does not maintain a separate online database containing player accounts or gameplay profiles for this Game.
