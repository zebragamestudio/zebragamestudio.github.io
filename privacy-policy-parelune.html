Zebra Game Studio (“we”, “us”, or “our”) operates the mobile game Parelune (the “Game”). This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you install and use the Game.

By using the Game, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Personal Information

The Game does not require registration or sign-in. We do not directly collect personally identifiable information such as your name, postal address, phone number, email address, or precise location through normal gameplay.

1.2 Technical Information

The Game may process basic technical information on your device as needed for compatibility and operation, such as device model, operating system version, Game version, language and display settings, and device-generated error information. The Game does not send this information to us for advertising or analytics.

1.3 Local Game Data

The Game may store the following information locally on your device:

  • Completed and unlocked routes and chapter progress
  • Recovered story pages
  • Daily Parcel completion and streak information
  • Gameplay results and current puzzle state
  • Sound, music, haptics, reduced motion, and other gameplay preferences

Local game data is used to maintain gameplay functionality and remember progress and settings. It is not uploaded to an online player database operated by us.

2. Game Progress and Local Storage

Parelune stores gameplay progress and preferences locally on your device. The Game does not require an online account or use a cloud save service, so progress may not automatically transfer between devices.

Uninstalling the Game or clearing its application data may permanently delete locally stored progress and settings, subject to your device's backup and restoration features.

3. Third-Party Services

The Game does not integrate advertising networks, external analytics services, social media SDKs, or third-party tracking tools such as Google AdMob, Firebase Analytics, Yandex Metrica, or the Facebook SDK.

The Game does not display advertisements or use third-party analytics tools to monitor or profile player behavior.

The app store and your device's operating system may independently process information under their own privacy policies when you download, install, or update the Game.

4. How Information Is Used

Information handled locally by the Game is used to operate the puzzles, save routes and chapter progress, unlock recovered pages, maintain Daily Parcel streaks, remember settings, and support device compatibility.

We do not sell, rent, trade, or use player information for targeted advertising.

5. Advertising and In-App Purchases

The Game does not contain advertisements or offer in-app purchases. We do not collect payment card details or billing information through the Game.

6. Accounts, Online Services, and Cloud Storage

The Game is designed to be played fully offline. It does not require an internet connection for normal gameplay, account registration, authentication, or cloud synchronization.

7. Data Sharing

We do not share personal information collected through the Game with advertisers, data brokers, or analytics providers. Locally stored progress and preferences are not routinely transmitted to servers operated by us.

We may respond to valid legal requests concerning any information actually in our possession, as required by applicable law.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Gameplay progress, recovered pages, Daily Parcel data, and settings may remain on your device while the Game's application data is retained. You can remove this data by clearing the Game's application data through your device settings or uninstalling the Game. Device backups may be managed separately by your operating system provider.

We do not maintain an online database of individual player profiles generated through normal gameplay.

9. Children’s Privacy

The Game is not specifically directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the Game. If you have concerns about a child's information in connection with the Game, contact us at the email address below.

10. Security

Gameplay data is primarily stored on your device and relies on the security features of your device and operating system. No method of electronic storage can be guaranteed completely secure.

11. Links to Other Websites

The Game does not require visits to external websites during normal gameplay. Any third-party websites linked from related pages operate under their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for their practices.

12. International Users

The Game may be available in multiple countries. Because normal gameplay is offline and no online player account database is operated by us, we do not routinely transfer player profiles to our servers.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to the Game or applicable requirements. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.
