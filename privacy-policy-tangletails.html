Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 10, 2026

Zebra Game Studio built TangleTails as a mobile word puzzle game. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected or processed when you use the app and how that information may be handled.

Information We Collect

TangleTails does not require account registration and does not ask you to provide personal information such as your name, phone number, postal address, or email address.

The app operates entirely offline and does not include analytics, advertising, crash reporting services, or other third-party data collection SDKs.

The app may access limited technical information provided by your device or operating system, which can include:

  • Device type
  • Operating system version
  • Language settings
  • Screen size and device configuration
  • Technical information necessary for app compatibility

How Information Is Used

Any limited technical information available to the app is used solely to ensure compatibility with supported devices, provide correct functionality, and improve future versions of the game.

TangleTails does not use device identifiers or Advertising IDs for analytics, advertising, profiling, or personalized content.

Offline Functionality

TangleTails is designed to work completely offline. No internet connection is required to play the game.

Game progress, unlocked trails, stars, discovered animals, Animal Album entries, Daily Trail progress, settings, and other gameplay information are stored locally on your device. Removing the app or clearing its storage may permanently delete this data.

Advertising

TangleTails does not display advertisements.

The game does not use Google AdMob or any other advertising platform. No personalized or non-personalized advertisements are shown.

Purchases and Payments

TangleTails does not contain in-app purchases, subscriptions, premium currencies, or any real-money transactions.

The app does not process payment information or Google Play Billing data.

Crash Reports and Analytics

TangleTails does not use Firebase Crashlytics, Firebase Analytics, Google Analytics, AppMetrica, or any similar analytics or crash-reporting services.

The developer does not receive gameplay analytics, behavior tracking information, or automatic crash reports from the app.

Information We Do Not Collect

  • No names or email addresses
  • No phone numbers
  • No account credentials
  • No precise or approximate location
  • No payment information
  • No contacts
  • No photos, videos, or audio recordings
  • No camera or microphone data
  • No messages or communication content
  • No browsing history
  • No biometric, financial, or health information

Permissions

TangleTails does not require access to your contacts, camera, microphone, location, photos, videos, or personal files for normal gameplay.

Third-Party Services

The app does not integrate third-party SDKs for advertising, analytics, user profiling, crash reporting, or behavioral tracking.

Your device operating system and the application distribution platform (such as Google Play) may independently process certain technical information according to their own privacy policies. Such processing is outside the control of Zebra Game Studio.

Data Sharing

Zebra Game Studio does not sell, rent, trade, or otherwise share user information with advertisers, analytics providers, marketing companies, or data brokers.

Information may only be disclosed where required by applicable law.

Data Retention

All gameplay progress remains stored locally on your device until you delete the app or clear its storage.

Because the app does not maintain online accounts or cloud storage, Zebra Game Studio does not retain gameplay progress on external servers.

Data Security

Although TangleTails stores data locally on your device, no electronic storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Reasonable measures are taken to provide a stable and secure application.

Children's Privacy

TangleTails does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults.

Since the app does not require registration and does not collect personal information, very little personal data can be associated with individual users.

Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your country or region, you may have rights regarding information processed about you.

Because TangleTails does not create user accounts or collect personal information, there is generally no personal account data available to access, modify, or delete.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes in the game, legal requirements, or privacy practices.

Any updates will be published on this page together with a revised effective date.
