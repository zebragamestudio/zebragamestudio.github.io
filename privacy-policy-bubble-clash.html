Zebra Game Studio (“we”, “us”, or “our”) operates the mobile game Prism Orbit: Bubble Clash (the “Game”).

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you install and use our Game.

By installing or using the Game, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Personal Information

The Game does not require users to create an account.

We do not directly collect personally identifiable information such as your name, postal address, phone number, email address, or precise location.

1.2 Technical Information

The Game may process basic technical information necessary for installation, compatibility, operation, and maintenance, such as:

  • Device model and manufacturer
  • Operating system and version
  • Game version
  • Language and display settings
  • Basic technical information related to application errors

This information is not used to create advertising profiles or track players across other applications or websites.

1.3 Local Game Data

The Game stores gameplay-related information locally on your device.

This information may include:

  • Current game progress
  • Unlocked difficulty levels
  • Best score and total score
  • Games played and games won
  • Win rate
  • Number of bubbles cleared
  • Largest combo
  • Perfect shots
  • Power-ups used
  • Earned stars
  • Unlocked visual themes
  • Selected visual theme
  • Favorite difficulty
  • Recent game results
  • Sound and music preferences
  • Vibration settings
  • Aim guide preferences
  • Accessibility and gameplay settings

This information is stored locally to provide game functionality, preserve progress, and remember your preferences.

Local gameplay information is not used to identify you personally.

2. Game Progress and Local Storage

Prism Orbit: Bubble Clash uses local storage on your device to remember your gameplay progress, statistics, unlocked difficulty levels, earned stars, visual themes, and selected settings.

The Game does not require an online account or cloud save service.

Progress stored on one device may therefore not automatically appear on another device.

If you uninstall the Game, reset your progress, or clear the application's data through your device settings, locally stored information may be permanently deleted.

3. Third-Party Services

The Game does not use advertising networks, external analytics services, social media SDKs, or third-party tracking tools.

The Game does not integrate advertising or analytics services such as Google AdMob, Firebase Analytics, Yandex Metrica, or Facebook SDK.

The Game does not display advertisements and does not use third-party analytics tools to monitor or profile player behavior.

4. How Information Is Used

Information handled by the Game may be used only for purposes related to its normal operation.

These purposes include:

  • Operating and maintaining the Game
  • Saving local gameplay progress
  • Remembering scores and statistics
  • Saving unlocked difficulty levels
  • Remembering earned stars and unlocked themes
  • Saving selected visual themes
  • Remembering sound and vibration preferences
  • Saving accessibility and gameplay settings
  • Maintaining compatibility with supported devices
  • Diagnosing and resolving technical problems

We do not sell, rent, trade, or use player information for targeted advertising.

5. Advertising and In-App Purchases

The Game does not contain advertisements.

The Game does not offer in-app purchases.

We do not collect, process, or store payment card details, billing information, or other financial information.

6. Data Sharing

We do not share personal information with advertisers, data brokers, or analytics providers.

Gameplay progress and preferences are stored locally on your device and are not uploaded to a separate online player database operated by us.

Information may be disclosed only when required by applicable law or a valid legal request.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Gameplay progress, statistics, stars, themes, scores, and settings may remain stored locally on your device while the Game is installed and its application data is retained.

You can remove locally stored gameplay information by using the reset option available in the Game.

You may also clear the Game's application data through your device settings or uninstall the Game.

Since the Game does not use an online account system, advertising service, or external analytics platform, we do not maintain a separate online database of individual player profiles.

8. Children's Privacy

Prism Orbit: Bubble Clash is not specifically directed at children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

The Game does not require account registration and does not request a player's name, email address, phone number, or precise location.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information in connection with the Game, please contact us so that we can review the matter and take appropriate action.

9. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the Game.

Gameplay information is primarily stored locally on the user's device and is protected by the security features of the device and operating system.

However, no method of electronic storage or data processing can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Links to Other Websites

The Game does not require users to visit external websites during normal gameplay.

If the Game or its related pages provide a link to a third-party website, that website is governed by its own terms and privacy policy.

We are not responsible for the content, security, or privacy practices of third-party websites.

11. International Users

The Game may be available to users in different countries.

Because gameplay information is primarily stored locally and the Game does not operate an online player account database, no routine transfer of personal gameplay profiles to our servers takes place.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the Game, its features, or applicable legal requirements change.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

We encourage users to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
