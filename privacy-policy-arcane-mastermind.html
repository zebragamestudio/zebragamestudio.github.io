Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 9, 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Arcane Mastermind, developed and published by Zebra Game Studio, handles user information.

We respect your privacy and aim to provide a safe, enjoyable, and transparent gaming experience.

Information We Do Not Collect

Arcane Mastermind does not require users to provide personal information to access or play the game.

We do not request the following information:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Home or physical address
  • Precise geographical location
  • Contacts or address book information
  • Financial or payment information
  • Account passwords or login credentials

Device Identifiers

The application may access or process certain technical identifiers associated with the user's device, including:

  • Advertising ID
  • Device ID or device-related identifiers

These identifiers may be processed for legitimate technical purposes related to application operation, compatibility, security, and device functionality.

The game does not use these identifiers to display personalized advertisements or create advertising profiles.

Advertising

Arcane Mastermind does not contain third-party advertisements.

The application does not integrate advertising networks such as Google AdMob or similar advertising platforms.

In-App Purchases

Arcane Mastermind does not offer in-app purchases, paid subscriptions, or other purchases within the application.

Offline Gameplay

Arcane Mastermind is designed to function offline.

An active internet connection is not required to solve puzzles, participate in daily challenges, or access the main gameplay features.

Accounts and Registration

The game does not require users to create an account, register, sign in, or provide authentication credentials.

No personal user profile is required to play.

Analytics and Third-Party Services

Arcane Mastermind does not integrate third-party analytics or advertising SDKs such as:

  • Google Firebase Analytics
  • Google AdMob
  • Facebook Analytics
  • Yandex Metrica
  • Other third-party advertising or analytics SDKs

We do not use third-party analytics services to track individual gameplay behavior or create user activity profiles.

Local Game Data

Arcane Mastermind may store gameplay-related information locally on the user's device.

This information may include:

  • Puzzle progress and completed challenges
  • Daily challenge results
  • Game scores and achievements
  • Winning streaks
  • Game history and statistics
  • Sound and vibration preferences

This information is used to maintain game progress, display statistics, and provide a consistent gameplay experience.

Gameplay progress is intended to remain stored locally on the device.

Daily Challenges

Arcane Mastermind includes daily puzzle challenges that allow players to test their logical thinking and track their progress over time.

Daily challenge results, streaks, and related statistics may be saved locally on the device.

These features do not require the creation of an online account.

Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal information.

Arcane Mastermind does not use third-party advertising or analytics services to share gameplay information for marketing purposes.

Data Retention and Deletion

Gameplay information stored locally on the device may remain available until the user clears the application's data or uninstalls the game.

The developer does not maintain an independent online database of personal player accounts for Arcane Mastermind.

Users may manage locally stored application data through their device settings.

Children's Privacy

Arcane Mastermind is a logic puzzle game that does not require users to submit personal information to play.

We do not knowingly solicit personal information from children under the age of 13.

If you believe that personal information relating to a child has been collected, please contact us using the email address below.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to maintain a secure and reliable application environment.

However, no electronic storage system or technical environment can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable privacy laws, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information.

You may manage application permissions, advertising identifiers, and locally stored application data through your device settings.

For privacy-related questions or requests, please contact the developer using the information provided below.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in the application, technical functionality, or applicable legal requirements.

Any changes will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
