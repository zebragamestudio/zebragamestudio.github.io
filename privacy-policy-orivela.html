Zebra Game Studio (“we”, “us”, or “our”) operates the mobile game Orivela: Ninth Fold (the “Game”).

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you install and use the Game.

By installing or using the Game, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Personal Information

Orivela: Ninth Fold does not require users to create an account or sign in.

We do not directly collect personally identifiable information through normal gameplay, including:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Postal or residential address
  • Precise geographical location
  • Contacts or address book information
  • Payment card or banking information
  • Authentication credentials

1.2 Technical Information

The Game may process basic technical information locally on your device when necessary for installation, compatibility, operation, and maintenance.

Such information may include:

  • Device model and manufacturer
  • Operating system and version
  • Game version
  • Language and display settings
  • Basic technical error information generated by the device

The Game does not use third-party advertising or analytics services to collect this information.

We do not use technical information to create advertising profiles or track players across other applications or websites.

1.3 Local Game Data

Orivela: Ninth Fold stores gameplay-related information locally on your device to provide its features, preserve progress, and remember your preferences.

Depending on how you use the Game, locally stored information may include:

  • Journey progress
  • Completed and unlocked Folds
  • Chapter completion progress
  • Unlocked Remembered Pages
  • Today's Fold completion status
  • Current and best daily streak information
  • Scores and gameplay results
  • Completion times
  • Mistake counts
  • Hint usage
  • Notes and active puzzle state
  • Sound effect preferences
  • Ambient sound preferences
  • Haptic preferences
  • Number highlighting preferences
  • Automatic note removal preference
  • Timer display preference
  • Mistake feedback preference
  • Hint confirmation preference
  • Other gameplay settings and preferences

This information is used only to provide Game functionality, preserve progress, calculate gameplay results, and remember selected preferences.

Locally stored gameplay information is not used to identify you personally and is not uploaded to an online player profile operated by Zebra Game Studio.

2. Game Progress and Local Storage

Orivela: Ninth Fold uses local storage on your device to remember Journey progress, completed and unlocked Folds, chapter progress, Remembered Pages, Today's Fold status, streak information, gameplay results, and selected settings.

The main Journey contains six chapters with twelve Folds in each chapter.

The Game stores progress locally so that completed puzzles, unlocked Folds, and story content remain available between play sessions.

The Game does not require an online player account and is designed to work offline.

Progress stored on one device may not automatically appear on another device.

If you uninstall the Game, clear its application data, reset your Journey using the available in-game option, or otherwise remove locally stored information, some or all progress and preferences may be permanently deleted.

3. Daily Puzzles

The Game includes a feature called Today's Fold, which provides a daily Sudoku puzzle based on the local day available to the Game on your device.

Information related to Today's Fold, including daily completion status and current and best streaks, may be stored locally on your device.

Daily puzzle information is used only to provide the daily gameplay feature and maintain your streak history.

4. Third-Party Services

The Game does not use advertising networks, external analytics services, social media SDKs, or third-party tracking tools such as Google AdMob, Firebase Analytics, Yandex Metrica, or the Facebook SDK for gameplay tracking or advertising purposes.

Orivela: Ninth Fold does not display advertisements and does not use third-party analytics tools to monitor, profile, or track player behavior.

5. How Information Is Used

Information handled locally by the Game may be used only for purposes necessary to provide and maintain its functionality, including:

  • Operating and maintaining the Game
  • Saving Journey and puzzle progress
  • Unlocking subsequent Folds after completed puzzles
  • Saving chapter completion progress
  • Unlocking and displaying Remembered Pages
  • Maintaining Today's Fold completion information
  • Maintaining current and best daily streaks
  • Calculating and displaying gameplay scores and results
  • Remembering active puzzle state and Notes
  • Remembering gameplay preferences and settings
  • Maintaining compatibility with supported devices
  • Diagnosing and resolving technical problems

We do not sell, rent, trade, or use player information for targeted advertising.

6. Advertising and In-App Purchases

Orivela: Ninth Fold does not contain third-party advertisements.

The Game does not offer in-app purchases.

The Game does not include real-money gameplay, wagering, cash prizes, or mechanisms for purchasing, withdrawing, or exchanging virtual items for real-world money.

We do not collect, process, or store payment card details, billing information, banking information, or other financial information through the Game.

7. Hints and Gameplay Assistance

The Game provides gameplay assistance features such as Notes, number highlighting, mistake feedback, and Hints.

Hints are free and unlimited.

They do not require payment, advertising interaction, or the purchase of virtual currency.

Information about Hint usage and gameplay results may be stored locally as part of puzzle statistics and progress.

8. Accounts, Online Services, and Offline Play

Orivela: Ninth Fold does not require account registration, authentication, or sign-in.

The Game is designed to be played completely offline.

An active internet connection is not required for normal Sudoku gameplay, Journey progression, Today's Fold, or Remembered Pages.

The Game does not rely on an online player profile or cloud-based synchronization service to maintain normal gameplay progress.

9. Data Sharing

We do not share personal information with advertisers, data brokers, analytics providers, or social media platforms through the Game.

Because normal gameplay progress and preferences are stored locally on your device, they are not routinely transmitted to a separate online player database operated by Zebra Game Studio.

Information may be disclosed only when required by applicable law, a valid legal request, or when reasonably necessary to protect legal rights and prevent misuse.

10. Data Retention and Deletion

Journey progress, completed Folds, unlocked Remembered Pages, Today's Fold information, streak data, puzzle results, Notes, and settings may remain stored locally on your device for as long as the Game remains installed and its application data is retained.

You can remove locally stored information by clearing the Game's application data through your device settings or by uninstalling the Game.

Certain progress may also be removed through available in-game reset features.

Since the Game does not use an online player account system, advertising service, external analytics platform, or online gameplay profile, we do not maintain a separate online database of individual player profiles generated through ordinary use of the Game.

11. Children's Privacy

Orivela: Ninth Fold is not specifically directed at children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the Game.

The Game does not require account registration and does not request a player's name, email address, phone number, postal address, or precise location during normal gameplay.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information in connection with the Game, please contact us so that we can review the matter and take appropriate action.

12. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the Game.

Gameplay progress and preferences are primarily stored locally on the user's device and are subject to the security features and protections provided by the device and its operating system.

However, no method of electronic storage or data processing can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

We are not responsible for the content, security, or privacy practices of third-party websites.

14. International Users

The Game may be available to users in different countries.

Because normal gameplay information is stored locally and the Game does not operate an online player profile database, no routine transfer of personal gameplay profiles to our servers takes place.
