1. Introduction

Welcome to Mystery of the Three Rings.

This Privacy Policy explains how Zebra Game Studio ("we", "our", or "us") handles information related to the mobile game Mystery of the Three Rings (the "App").

We aim to minimize the collection of personal information.

Such information may include:

Zebra Game Studio does not sell users' personal information.
