Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 9, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to CodeMind: Color Code, a mobile puzzle game developed and published by Zebra Game Studio.

At Zebra Game Studio, we respect your privacy and are committed to providing a safe and transparent gaming experience.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use CodeMind: Color Code.

The game is designed to operate offline and does not require users to create an account or provide personal information.

2. Information We Collect

CodeMind: Color Code does not directly collect, store, or transmit personally identifiable information.

The game does not request or collect:

  • Full names or usernames
  • Email addresses
  • Phone numbers
  • Physical addresses
  • Precise location information
  • Contacts or address book information
  • Photos, videos, or personal media
  • Financial or payment information
  • Login credentials or passwords

No registration or personal account is required to access the game.

3. Game Progress and Local Storage

CodeMind: Color Code may store certain gameplay-related information locally on your device to support game functionality and maintain your progress.

This information may include:

  • Player level and experience points (XP)
  • Completed puzzles and game statistics
  • Wins and winning streaks
  • Achievements and daily mission progress
  • Assist Tokens and in-game rewards
  • Sound, vibration, and accessibility preferences

This information is used exclusively to provide gameplay features and improve the local gaming experience.

Game progress is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to Zebra Game Studio's servers.

Removing the application or clearing its stored data may result in the loss of saved game progress.

4. Internet Connection

CodeMind: Color Code is designed to work offline.

An active internet connection is not required to play the game, solve puzzles, complete missions, or access its main features.

The game does not require an online account or cloud-based gameplay services.

5. Advertising

CodeMind: Color Code does not display advertisements.

The game does not integrate advertising networks for the purpose of displaying ads or tracking users for advertising.

6. In-App Purchases

CodeMind: Color Code does not offer in-app purchases.

Experience points, Assist Tokens, achievements, and other gameplay rewards are earned through normal gameplay and available in-game activities.

No real-money transactions are required to access the game's features.

7. Analytics and Tracking

CodeMind: Color Code does not use third-party analytics services to monitor player behavior.

The game does not use advertising identifiers, behavioral profiling, or tracking technologies for marketing purposes.

8. Third-Party Services

CodeMind: Color Code does not intentionally share personal information with third-party advertising or analytics providers.

The application may be distributed through third-party application stores, such as Google Play.

These platforms may independently process information according to their own privacy policies and terms of service.

Zebra Game Studio does not control the privacy practices of external platforms.

9. Children's Privacy

CodeMind: Color Code does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

The game does not require users to submit personal information or create an account.

If you are a parent or guardian and have concerns regarding your child's privacy, please contact us using the email address provided below.

10. Data Security

We value the security and privacy of our players.

Because CodeMind: Color Code does not directly collect personal information and stores gameplay progress locally, the amount of information processed by the developer is minimized.

Users are encouraged to maintain appropriate security settings on their devices.

11. Data Retention and Deletion

Gameplay information stored locally on your device remains available until it is removed through the device's application management settings.

You may delete locally stored game data by clearing the application's storage or uninstalling the game.

Zebra Game Studio does not maintain a separate online database containing individual player progress.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

Zebra Game Studio may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the game, applicable requirements, or privacy practices.

Any updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date.

We encourage users to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
