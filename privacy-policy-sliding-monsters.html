Privacy Policy – Sliding Monsters

Effective date: October 9, 2026

Zebra Game Studio ("we", "us", or "our") provides the mobile game Sliding Monsters (the "Game"). This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when you use the Game and how it is handled.

1. Information We Collect

The Game does not require you to create an account or provide your name, email address, or other contact details to play. However, the Game may collect or process the following technical information:

  • Advertising ID: the advertising identifier associated with your device, where available.
  • Device ID: device identifiers and related technical device information.
  • Crash logs: diagnostic information about errors, crashes, and app performance.

2. How Information Is Used

Technical information may be used to maintain the Game, diagnose crashes, troubleshoot technical issues, protect the integrity of the app, and improve stability and performance. Although an Advertising ID may be processed, the Game does not display advertisements.

3. Offline Gameplay

Sliding Monsters can be played offline, including its Daily Mayhem mode. Internet access is not required for core gameplay. Offline availability does not necessarily mean that technical information is never transmitted when a connection is available.

4. Advertising and In-App Purchases

The Game does not contain advertisements or in-app purchases.

5. Sharing and Service Providers

Technical information may be processed by service providers that support diagnostics, app stability, or related technical operations, if such services are integrated into the Game. We do not sell personal information. Any applicable providers should handle data under their own privacy terms and relevant legal requirements.

6. Data Retention and Security

We aim to keep technical information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy, subject to applicable legal obligations and service-provider retention practices. We take reasonable steps to protect information, but no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

7. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly request names, email addresses, or other contact details from children through the Game. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can review the matter and take appropriate action.

8. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on your device and applicable law, you may be able to reset or limit access to your Advertising ID through your device settings. You may contact us with questions or requests concerning personal information associated with the Game. We will review requests in accordance with applicable law.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to the Game, technical practices, or legal requirements. Any updated version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
