Privacy Policy

Game: Invasion of Beasts
Developer: Zebra Game Studio
Effective Date: October 9, 2026

1. Introduction

Zebra Game Studio ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Invasion of Beasts handles information when you play it.

2. Information Collection

Invasion of Beasts is designed to be played offline without registration or a user account. The game does not intentionally collect or transmit personal information to Zebra Game Studio, such as your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, or payment details.

3. Local Game Data

Game progress, unlocked levels, scores, settings, and upgrade information may be stored locally on your device to support gameplay. This information is used for game functionality and is not sent to our servers. Removing the game or clearing its app data may erase locally saved progress.

4. Internet Connectivity

The game does not require an internet connection for its core gameplay. No online account or cloud synchronization is required.

5. Advertising and In-App Purchases

Invasion of Beasts does not display advertisements and does not offer in-app purchases. In-game points and tokens are earned through gameplay and are used for in-game upgrades.

6. Analytics and Third-Party Services

We do not use advertising or analytics services to track players. The game is distributed through an app marketplace, which may independently process information under its own privacy terms. Such marketplace processing is separate from the game's offline functionality.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell or share players' personal information with advertisers or data brokers. We do not operate a server that receives gameplay data from the game.

8. Data Security, Retention, and Deletion

Because gameplay information is stored locally, its protection depends in part on your device's security. We do not retain personal gameplay data on our servers. You can generally remove locally stored game data through your device's app settings or by uninstalling the game. Device backup features, if enabled, may be managed separately by your operating system provider.

9. Children's Privacy

The game does not require players to provide personal information or create an account. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the game. If you have concerns about children's privacy, please contact us.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the game or its data practices change. Any revised version will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
