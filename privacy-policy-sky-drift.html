Zebra Game Studio (“we”, “us”, or “our”) operates the mobile game Sky Drift (the “Game”).

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you install and use our Game.

By installing or using the Game, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Personal Information

The Game does not require users to create an account.

We do not directly collect personally identifiable information such as:

  • Full name
  • Home address
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Payment information

1.2 Device Information

The Game may process limited technical information related to your device, such as:

  • Device model and manufacturer
  • Operating system and version
  • Application version
  • Technical information required for compatibility

Such information is used only as necessary for basic application functionality, compatibility, stability, and maintenance.

1.3 Local Game Data

Sky Drift may store gameplay-related information locally on your device, including:

  • Current and best scores
  • Unlocked levels
  • Level progress
  • Selected character skin
  • Sound preferences
  • Vibration preferences
  • Other gameplay settings

This information is stored locally and is not used by us to identify you personally.

2. Game Progress and Local Storage

Sky Drift uses local device storage to remember your gameplay progress and preferences.

This includes your best score, unlocked levels, selected character appearance, and settings.

If you uninstall the Game, reset your progress, or clear application data, locally stored information may be deleted.

3. Third-Party Services

The Game does not integrate third-party advertising networks, analytics services, or social media tracking SDKs.

We do not use services such as:

  • Google AdMob
  • Firebase Analytics
  • Facebook SDK
  • Yandex Metrica
  • Other third-party advertising or tracking tools

Sky Drift does not display advertisements or use third-party analytics to track player behavior.

4. How We Use Information

Information described in this Privacy Policy may be processed for the following purposes:

  • To operate and maintain the Game
  • To save gameplay progress
  • To remember best scores
  • To manage unlocked levels
  • To remember selected character skins
  • To store sound and vibration preferences
  • To maintain compatibility and stability
  • To identify and resolve technical issues

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information to third parties.

5. Advertising and In-App Purchases

Sky Drift does not contain advertisements.

The Game does not offer in-app purchases.

We do not collect or store payment card details or other financial information.

6. Data Retention

Gameplay information, including best scores, unlocked levels, selected skins, and settings, may remain stored locally on your device.

You can remove this information by using the available reset function, clearing application data, or uninstalling the Game.

We do not maintain a separate online user database for Sky Drift.

7. Children's Privacy

Sky Drift is not specifically directed at children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the Game, please contact us so that we can take appropriate action.

8. Security

We value user privacy and take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the Game.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Links to Other Sites

Sky Drift does not require access to external websites for normal gameplay.

If external links are provided, we are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of third-party websites.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

Any changes will be published on this page, together with an updated revision date.

We encourage users to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
